      Players

          How to play Old Maid
          • The Q♣ is removed, so one queen can never be paired: that's the Old Maid.
          • Cards are dealt clockwise; a random player goes first. Pairs are discarded automatically.
          • On your turn, take a hidden card from the next player who has cards, or draw from the draw pile.
          • Once the draw pile is empty, anyone without cards is safe. Whoever is left holding the Old Maid loses.
          How to play Pusoy Dos
          • 2 to 4 players. Each gets 13 cards (17 with three players); any others are set aside unseen.
          • Cards rank 3 (lowest) up to K, A, 2 (highest). Suits break ties: ♣ < ♠ < ♥ < ♦.
          • Whoever has the 3♣ (or the lowest card dealt) leads, and the first play must include that card.
          • Play a single, a pair, three of a kind, or five cards: straight < flush < full house < four of a kind (+ any card) < straight flush.
          • Straights run from 3-4-5-6-7 up to 10-J-Q-K-A (no 2s, no wrapping round) and, like flushes, are compared by their highest card. Full houses and four of a kind are compared by the matching cards.
          • On your turn, beat the cards on the table with a higher play of the same number of cards, or pass. You can still play on a later turn after passing.
          • When everyone else passes, the last player to play clears the table and leads anything.
          • The first player to get rid of all their cards wins.
          How to play Go Fish
          • 2 to 10 players, full 52-card deck. Everyone gets 7 cards (5 with four or more players); the rest form the pile.
          • On your turn, ask any player for a rank you hold. If they have any, they hand them all over and you go again.
          • If not, go fish: draw from the pile. Draw the rank you asked for and you go again; otherwise the turn passes.
          • Four of a kind make a book, laid down automatically. Run out of cards and you draw one on your turn.
          • When all 13 books are made, whoever has the most wins.
