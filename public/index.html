                Old Maid options

                Old Maid uses the 52-card deck minus the Q♣. Cards that aren't dealt form the draw pile.

                Mafia options

                Talk in person or on a call: the app deals the roles, runs the night and counts the votes.

                  How to play
                  • The Q♣ is removed, so one queen can never be paired: that's the Old Maid.
                  • Cards are dealt clockwise; a random player goes first. Pairs are discarded automatically.
                  • On your turn, take a hidden card from the next player who has cards, or draw from the draw pile.
                  • Once the draw pile is empty, anyone without cards is safe. Whoever is left holding the Old Maid loses.
                  • Everyone gets a secret card. An ace means you're Mafia (the Mafia know each other), the Q♥ is the Doctor, the K♠ is the Detective, and a number card is a Villager.
                  • At night everyone picks someone. The Mafia's choice is killed unless the Doctor protected them (never the same person two nights running). The Detective learns whether their pick is Mafia. Villagers' picks do nothing; they just hide who has a role.
                  • By day, talk it over and vote. The player with the most votes is out, unless it's a tie or "nobody" has as many votes. The cards of anyone who's out are shown.
                  • The town wins when every Mafia member is out. The Mafia win once they match the town in number.
